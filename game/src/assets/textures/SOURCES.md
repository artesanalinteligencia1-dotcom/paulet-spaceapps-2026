# Earth textures — sources

All three files are NASA imagery (public domain, no logos), downloaded on 2026-11-14 and resized for the game.
Credit in the game and on the project page: "Earth imagery: NASA Earth Observatory / Visible Earth — Blue Marble: Next
Generation (2004) and Black Marble (2016)".

| File | Size | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `earth-day-4k.jpg` | 4096 × 2048 | Blue Marble: Next Generation with Topography and Bathymetry, July 2004 (`world.topo.bathy.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg`), https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/ |
| `earth-day-1k.jpg` | 1024 × 512 | Same image, smaller (map view and small globes) |
| `earth-night-4k.jpg` | 4096 × 2048 | Black Marble 2016 global composite (`BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg`, 13500 × 6750), NASA Earth Observatory image record 144898 |

Equirectangular projection: longitude −180° at the left edge, latitude +90° at the top.
